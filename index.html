Islamabad,The Beautiful

Islamabad, the master-planned capital of Pakistan, is widely celebrated as one of the most scenic and green capital cities in the world. Conceived in 1960 and established in 1967 to replace Karachi, the city is nestled against the lush, tree-lined foothills of the Margalla Hills National Park. Its layout is organized into clean, systematic sectors dotted with parks, shaded walkways, and vibrant seasonal flora like jacarandas and bougainvilleas. Iconic landmarks such as the Bedouin-tent inspired Faisal Mosque, the petal-shaped Pakistan Monument, and scenic viewpoints like Daman-e-Koh blend modern architecture with a calm, nature-integrated lifestyle.
